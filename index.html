How it works

One tap, once a day

  1. Open it in the evening

    Today’s entry is the first thing you see.

  2. Rate the day from 0 to 10

    Tap or drag along the scale, from worst day to best day.

  3. Add energy if you like

    Five levels, from drained to charged. Nothing else to fill in: no notes, no tags, no photos.

What you get

Small habit, clear picture

One tap a day

One entry per calendar day: a mood from 0 to 10, plus energy from 1 to 5 if you want it. Missed a day? Fill it in later.

Your year in colour

A month calendar and a year grid, one square per day, in the colour you pick: green, amber, violet or blue.

Streaks and insights

Your current and longest streak, averages by weekday and a year in review, all worked out on your phone.

Private by default

Your entries live on your phone. Signing in and cloud backup are both optional, and backup stays off until you turn it on.

What it costs

Free, with ads

Tint is free to use. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and the app asks for your consent where the law requires it.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tint works without signing in. An account is only needed if you want cloud backup.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything works offline except ads and cloud backup.

Where are my entries stored?

On your phone. If you turn on cloud backup, a copy is also kept on Tint’s own server in Vietnam. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I export my entries?

Not yet.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all entries clears your phone. To delete your account and any backed-up entries, see Delete account.
