How it works

Delete your Tint account

Last updated: 15 November 2026

A Tint account is optional. It holds your sign-in identity (name, email, profile photo, account identifier) and, only if you turned on cloud backup, a copy of your mood entries on our server.

In the app

  1. Open Tint → Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

Email contact@vtrera.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Tint account". We delete the account and its backed-up entries within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and its identifiers at Google Firebase Authentication.
  • Every entry synced to our server under that account, removed for good. Copies in the server's daily backups are deleted within 14 days.

Both routes delete the same things. Subscription purchase records are kept by Google Play/Apple and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax; deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

Entries on your phone

Deleting your account does not delete the entries on your phone. They go when you use Settings → Delete all entries, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Unless cloud backup was on, they were never on our server, so the phone was their only copy.
