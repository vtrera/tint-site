Support

Contact

Email contact@vtrera.com. Please include:

  • your phone model and its Android or iOS version;
  • the Tint version, shown at the bottom of the app's Settings screen;
  • what you did, what happened, and what you expected to happen.

Tint is made by one person, so a reply usually takes a few days.

Questions

My entries are gone after reinstalling

Tint keeps your entries on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them, and if cloud backup was off they were never stored anywhere else, so they cannot be recovered. If backup was on, sign in again with the same account and turn on Settings → Cloud sync → Sync my entries: your entries come back from the server.

Reminders are not arriving

Check that notifications for Tint are allowed in your phone's settings; if they are off, Tint's Settings shows "Notifications are off in system settings" with a link to fix it. Some phones also stop notifications from apps they put to sleep to save battery: turn off battery optimisation for Tint. Tint plans reminders a week ahead and refreshes them each time you open it, so open the app at least once a week.

Restoring a purchase

In Tint, open Settings → Upgrade to Premium and tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought with. To manage or cancel a subscription, use your store account settings.

Deleting my account

See Delete account for the in-app steps and how to ask by email.
