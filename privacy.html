How it works

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what the Tint app ("Tint", "we") collects and why. Tint is published by Vtrera Labs, an independent developer based in Vietnam, who is the data controller. Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Your mood entries stay on your device unless you turn on backup

Your daily ratings, settings and reminder times are stored on your phone. Unless you turn on cloud backup (below), they are not uploaded to us or to anyone else, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Cloud backup (optional)

Cloud backup is off until you turn it on. It needs a signed-in account, and signing in alone uploads nothing: you turn it on yourself in Settings → Cloud sync → Sync my entries.

While it is on, Tint sends a copy of your entries over HTTPS to a server that we run ourselves, located in Vietnam. For each day, the server stores:

  • the date;
  • your mood rating (0–10);
  • your energy level (1–5), if you logged one;
  • the time you made the edit, and the time the server received it;
  • a marker for a day you cleared, so your other devices clear it too.

Each entry is tied to your sign-in account identifier, so only you can read it back, on any device where you sign in with the same account. Nothing else from the app is uploaded: no settings, no reminder times. Your ratings are never written to the server's logs.

Turning backup off with "Turn off and erase", or deleting your account, removes every one of your entries from the server. Turning backup off without erasing stops new uploads and keeps your server copy. The server's database is also copied once a day for disaster recovery, and each copy is deleted after 14 days, so erased entries are gone from those copies within 14 days. There is no other expiry.

Optional sign-in

Signing in is optional; Tint works fully without an account. If you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", Google Firebase Authentication receives your name, email address, profile photo URL and an account identifier from that provider. We use them only to show who is signed in, to link your subscription to your account and, if you turn on cloud backup, to keep your backed-up entries apart from everyone else's. Your mood entries are attached to your account only while cloud backup is on.

Ads

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads, and, with your consent where the law requires it, to personalise them. You are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later in the app's Settings. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Google Play (or the App Store) and RevenueCat, which receive a purchase record and an anonymous app user identifier (or your account identifier if you are signed in) so your subscription can be restored. We never see your payment details.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No data is sent to show them.

No analytics

Tint contains no analytics and no crash-reporting service.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google Firebase, Google AdMob, RevenueCat, Google Play/Apple) for the purposes described. Backed-up entries are not shared with any of them. Data sent to them, and to our server, is encrypted in transit.

Deleting your data

  • Entries on your phone: Settings → Delete all entries, or uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Entries on our server: Settings → Cloud sync → Turn off and erase.
  • Your account and everything on our server: Settings → Account → Delete account. See Delete account for how to request deletion without the app.

Children

Tint is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes, and change the date above.
